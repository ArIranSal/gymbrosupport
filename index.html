Frequently asked questions

What is GymBro?

GymBro is an AI-powered social macro tracker. You log meals by photo, text, voice or barcode, and your friends can see your progress so you keep each other consistent. It runs on the web today, with iOS and Android apps coming soon.

Is it free?

Yes. The core tracker, friends, streaks and calendar are free. Pro adds deeper nutrition analysis and more advanced tracking features.

Do I need a gym partner to use it?

No. GymBro works as a solo tracker. It works considerably better with at least one friend who can see your logs, which is why the invite is the first thing we show you.

How do I log a meal?

Open the Camera tab. Snap a photo of the plate, type a description, say it out loud, or scan a barcode. GymBro names the dish, estimates calories and macros, and shows you a draft. Adjust anything that looks off, then tap Log.

How accurate is photo tracking?

The model estimates calories and macros from a photo, and it is an estimate rather than a lab measurement. Mixed meals and hidden ingredients are the hard cases. Adding a note about portion size or how it was cooked tightens the number, and every value is editable before you log it.

Can I edit or delete a meal after logging it?

Yes. Open the meal from your Home or Friends feed to change any number or remove it. Your rings and calendar update straight away.

Can I log water and supplements?

Water has its own ring on the Home tab with quick-add buttons. Supplements are logged from the Log a supplement button under your rings, and they appear alongside your meals.

How do I add a friend?

Go to your Profile and tap the QR code. Your friend scans it with their camera, or you scan theirs. You can also share the link it generates.

How do streaks work?

A day counts when you log your meals and stay on track with your targets. Your current streak and best streak are on your Profile, and every day shows on the Calendar as a hit, a cheat day or a miss.

What are cheat tokens?

Cheat tokens let you plan a day off without breaking your streak. You get a set number each month. Use one on a day you know you will go over, and the calendar marks it as a cheat day rather than a miss.

Who can see my logs?

Only the friends you have added. Friends are added by invitation, and you can remove a friend at any time from the Friends tab.

How do I set my calorie and macro targets?

Open Profile, then tap Edit under Macro and Water Targets. Set calories, protein, carbs, fat, sugar and water. Everything on Home tracks against those numbers.

Can I switch between metric and imperial?

Yes. The toggle is on the Home tab next to your rings. It changes units everywhere in the app.

I forgot my password.

On the log in page, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We will send a reset link. Check spam if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

How do I change my email or name?

Open Profile and tap the settings icon. Name and email can be changed there.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Not yet. GymBro runs in your phone's browser at app.gymbroweb.com, and you can add it to your home screen from the browser's share menu. Native apps for the App Store and Google Play are on the way.

What does Pro add?

Pro adds deeper nutrition analysis and the more advanced tracking features on top of the free tier. Current pricing and the full feature list live in the app under Profile.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Profile, then Manage subscription. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the period you have paid for, and nothing is deleted.

Can I get a refund?

Send a request below with the email on your account and we will sort it out. We are a small team, so you will hear from a real person.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Send a request below from the email on your account and we will export your history or delete your account and its data.

Do you sell my data?

No. Your logs and photos are used to run the app and nothing else.

Photo scan is not recognising my food.

Good light and a top-down angle help most. If the dish is layered or mixed, add a short note describing it before you scan. You can also switch to text or voice and describe the meal instead.

My streak reset and I think it should not have.

Check the Calendar for the day in question. If it shows a miss and you logged that day, send a request below with the date and we will look at it.
